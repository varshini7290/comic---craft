A Mystery Comic Story

Chapter 1: The Beginning

1. A Strange Beginning at Midnight

At midnight, a young girl heard a strange sound outside her room. She looked through the window and noticed something unusual near the old house.

2. Frightened by the Window Shadow

Suddenly, a dark shadow appeared near the window. She became curious and slowly moved closer to see who was standing outside.

3. The Secret Door

While exploring the house, she discovered a hidden door behind an old bookshelf. She opened it and found a mysterious room.

4. The Shocking Truth

Inside the room, she found an old photograph and a secret letter. The truth revealed that someone had been hiding a secret for many years.

5. The Terrifying Secret

Just when she thought everything was over, she heard footsteps behind her. She turned around and discovered that the real mystery had only just begun.
